Our process

Five stages. One consistent standard.

Every item follows the same structured path from your hands to its sealed holder and back.

  1. 1

    Submit

    Send your item safely to us, or hand it over in person in Baku.

  2. 2

    Authenticate

    We verify authenticity using magnification, lighting and reference comparison.

  3. 3

    Grade

    Graders evaluate condition against published criteria for the category.

  4. 4

    Encapsulate

    The item is sealed in a tamper-evident GLA holder with its printed label.

  5. 5

    Return

    Registered in our database, then packed and shipped back to you protected.
