Our services

Grading and authentication, by category

Each collectible type is judged on the criteria that actually matter for it. Below is exactly what we accept, and exactly what we look at.

Trading cards Sports cards Comic books Video games Diecast & toys Coins Banknotes Autographs

Trading card grading

Professional grading for modern and vintage TCG and CCG collections, sealed in a premium GLA slab with a printed label and unique certification number.

Pokémon One Piece Yu-Gi-Oh! Magic: The Gathering Disney Lorcana Dragon Ball TCG & CCG

Every card is evaluated for

  • Centering, front and back
  • Corner sharpness
  • Edge wear and chipping
  • Surface: print lines, scratches, dimples
  • Overall eye appeal

Sports card grading

Rookies, parallels, autographed inserts and vintage issues, graded on the same four-factor standard used for trading cards.

Basketball Football Baseball Soccer UFC Formula 1 Hockey

What we check

  • Centering, corners, edges and surface
  • Refractor and foil surface integrity
  • On-card autograph condition and placement
  • Patch and relic window condition
  • Serial numbering legibility

Comic book grading

Professional comic grading with archival protection. Books are handled with clean tools and sealed in an archival case sized to the issue.

Golden & Silver Age Modern keys Variants Manga volumes

We inspect

  • Cover gloss, colour and wear
  • Spine integrity, stress and roll
  • Page quality and colour
  • Corners, creases and tears
  • Restoration and colour-touch detection

Video game grading

Factory-sealed and complete-in-box titles, graded with a separate seal rating so buyers can judge the wrap independently of the box.

PlayStation Xbox Nintendo Sega Atari PC

Evaluation includes

  • Seal integrity and wrap type
  • Box corners, crushing and colour fade
  • Manual and insert condition
  • Disc or cartridge condition
  • Authenticity of the seal and print run

Diecast & Hot Wheels grading

Carded and loose diecast graded on presentation as a whole — the card and blister matter as much as the casting.

Hot Wheels Matchbox Mini GT Tomica Auto World Limited editions

Inspection includes

  • Card condition, corners and creases
  • Blister clarity, lifting and yellowing
  • Paint quality and tampo alignment
  • Factory defects and wheel alignment
  • Overall presentation

Coin grading

Authentication and grading of collectible coins, sealed in an inert capsule that protects the surface without altering the toning.

Commemorative Circulated Bullion World coins

Evaluation includes

  • Strike quality and detail
  • Surface preservation and marks
  • Luster and toning
  • Wear on the highest points
  • Authenticity, weight and dimensions

Banknote grading

Professional grading for collectible paper money, sealed in an archival sleeve that keeps the note flat and readable from both sides.

Historic issues Commemorative Replacement notes Low serials

Inspection includes

  • Paper quality, body and crispness
  • Centering of the printed design
  • Folds, handling and pinholes
  • Stains, foxing and ink transfer
  • Print quality and security features

Autograph authentication

Authentication for signed collectibles, issued with a GLA Certificate of Authenticity and a permanent record in our database.

Movie posters Photos Funko Pops Trading cards Jerseys Comic books Memorabilia

For selected events we also offer Witnessed Signature Authentication — a GLA representative watches the item being signed and documents it on the spot. Read how witnessed authentication works.

What authentication covers

  • Signature comparison against reference exemplars
  • Ink, medium and flow analysis
  • Substrate and item authenticity
  • Provenance review where available
  • Certificate of Authenticity with QR verification
