About GLA

Built for collectors who take their collections seriously

Grading Lab Agency was founded with one vision: to create a trusted grading and authentication service for collectors in Azerbaijan and beyond.

Why we exist

Collecting here deserved a local standard

For years, a collector in Baku with a card, comic or sealed game worth protecting had two options: ship it thousands of kilometres and wait months, or accept that its condition and authenticity would always be a matter of opinion.

GLA exists to close that gap. We combine expert evaluation, modern inspection technology and a documented method so that every collectible receives the care and attention it deserves — without leaving the region.

Every item submitted to GLA goes through the same structured evaluation before receiving its final grade or authentication result. No shortcuts for volume, no exceptions for familiar faces.
