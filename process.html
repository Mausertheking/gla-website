How grading works

Seven stages, documented end to end

No item skips a step. Each stage is recorded against your submission so you can see exactly where your collectible is and how it was assessed.

The grading desk

Step into the room where the grade is decided

Five instruments do the work, in this order. Click any of them — on the desk or in the list below it — to see exactly what that tool is looking for.

Step 1 of 5

Lint-free cotton gloves

Worn before anything is touched

Skin transfers oil, salt and moisture that no cleaning process can reverse. Every item is handled with fresh gloves from the moment the parcel is opened until the holder is sealed.

What it prevents

  • Fingerprint etching in coin luster
  • Oil haze on foil, holo and refractor surfaces
  • Long-term discolouration on comic pages
  • Residue trapped under the seal at encapsulation
  • Transfer between items in a bulk submission
Stage by stage

Seven steps, every submission

  1. 1

    Submit your collectible

    Complete a submission form, pack the item using our packaging guidance and send it to us — or hand it over in person in Baku. Every parcel is logged and photographed on arrival.

  2. 2

    Authentication inspection

    Before condition is considered at all, we confirm the item is what it claims to be: print characteristics, materials, seals, dimensions and known counterfeit markers.

  3. 3

    Professional grading

    A grader evaluates the item against the published criteria for its category under controlled lighting and magnification, recording each sub-factor separately.

  4. 4

    Quality control review

    A second reviewer independently checks the assessment. Where the two disagree, the item is re-examined before any grade is finalised.

  5. 5

    Secure encapsulation

    The item is sealed in a tamper-evident GLA holder sized for its category, with a printed label carrying the description, grade and certification number.

  6. 6

    Certification & online registration

    High-resolution images and the full grading record are added to the GLA database, and the certification number becomes verifiable online and by QR code.

  7. 7

    Return shipment

    The graded item is packed in protective material and shipped back insured, with tracking shared as soon as it leaves us.

The GLA scale

A 10-point scale, applied the same way every time

Numeric grades run from 10 down to 1. Card and comic grades combine centering, corners, edges and surface; the lowest sub-factor carries the most weight.

GLA numeric grades and what they describe.
Grade Designation What it means
10Gem MintVirtually flawless. Sharp corners, clean edges, full gloss and centering within the tightest tolerance.
9.5Mint+Gem quality held back by one very minor factor, usually centering.
9MintOne small flaw visible on close inspection: a light edge nick or slight print imperfection.
8Near Mint–MintMinor wear on one or two factors; still visually strong from a normal viewing distance.
7Near MintLight handling evident — slight corner softness or a shallow surface scuff.
6Excellent–Near MintNoticeable but modest wear; no creasing, no significant surface damage.
5ExcellentVisible corner and edge wear, minor surface marks, presentable overall.
4Very Good–ExcellentRounded corners, edge wear and light creasing that does not break the surface.
3Very GoodClear handling wear, creasing or scuffing; item remains complete and intact.
2GoodHeavy wear, multiple creases, possible small tears or staining.
1Poor–FairSevere damage. Preserved and certified for authenticity and history rather than condition.
Qualifiers

When a grade carries a note

Restoration, trimming, colour touch, authentic-altered and similar findings are recorded as qualifiers on the label rather than hidden inside the number.

Authenticity only

Items we authenticate without grading

Autographed memorabilia and certain oversized items receive an authentication result and a Certificate of Authenticity instead of a numeric grade.

Not genuine

If an item fails authentication

It is not encapsulated and not assigned a grade. We return it with a written explanation of what we found, at no grading charge.

Before you submit

Common questions

How should I package my item?

Put cards in a penny sleeve inside a semi-rigid holder — never a screw-down or top-loader taped shut. Comics go in a bag and board between two rigid sheets. Sealed games and diecast should be wrapped in bubble and suspended away from the box walls. Use a sturdy outer box with at least 5 cm of padding on every side.

Can I clean or press my item first?

Please don't. Cleaning, pressing and trimming are detectable and will be recorded as alterations, which lowers the grade or fails the item outright. Send it exactly as it is.

What happens if I disagree with a grade?

You can request a review. The item is re-examined by a grader who did not work on the original assessment. If the review finds an error, the grade is corrected and the item is re-encapsulated at no cost to you.

Is my item insured while you have it?

Yes. Items are insured from check-in to the moment they are handed to the return carrier. Declare an accurate value on your submission form so cover matches what you sent.

Do you grade items you cannot authenticate?

No. Authentication always comes first. If we cannot confirm an item is genuine, it is returned unencapsulated with our findings explained in writing.
