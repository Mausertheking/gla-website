Reading the holder

Where to find your certification number

Each GLA holder carries the same four pieces of information, in the same places.

Label description

The item name, set, year and any variant, printed across the top of the label.

Grade & designation

The numeric grade with its designation — for example 10 Gem Mint — on the right.

Certification number

A unique number in the format GLA0000000, never reissued to another item.

QR code

Scanning it opens this page with the record already loaded — no typing required.

If something looks wrong

A number that does not verify is a warning sign

Counterfeit holders exist for every major grading company. The certification number is the check that cannot be faked — because it either exists in our database or it does not.

Report a suspicious item

Before you buy, check that

  • The certification number verifies on this page
  • The item shown in the record matches what you are looking at
  • The grade on the label matches the grade in the record
  • The holder seal is intact, with no cuts, glue or reheating marks
  • The label is printed cleanly, not a photocopy or inkjet reproduction
